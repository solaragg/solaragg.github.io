FAQ

We’ve got you covered.

Where’s the official server?

We don’t have a Discord at the moment. We’re getting that sorted out legally before we run one again. In the meantime our community lives on Stoat.

We were also wrongfully terminated from Telegram, and there are no plans to restart a channel there. Anything else claiming to be an official Solara server is not run by us.

How much sUNC / UNC?

59% sUNC, 76% UNC.

The sUNC results are public: read the full report.

Because Solara is external, that ceiling is lower than a paid internal executor’s. Most popular scripts run fine; some will not.

Why is this free?

Solara was pretty much a passion project, so money wasn’t in mind. It’s very easy to bring updates, and keeping Solara running does not cost any money.

The only monetization at the moment is the Linkvertise on the download link, which is really simple and fast, and you keep the auto-updating software forever.

How do I contact the developer?

By email at cmdsoftworks@proton.me, on Telegram at @quiving, or in the Stoat server.

Do you collect anything from me?

Not during normal use. Writing scripts, running them, changing your theme: none of that is collected or sent anywhere.

Solara does have protections against malicious scripts and vulnerabilities, and if those trigger we may see a small amount of information so we can patch the problem.

Where is it safe to download Solara?

Only from our official release mirrors.

Reuploads on random sites and YouTube descriptions are the usual way people end up with malware while thinking they installed Solara.

Intended use

Solara is intended for developers testing and pentesting their own games and experiences. Using it against games you do not own may violate the Roblox Terms of Use and can get your account moderated.
